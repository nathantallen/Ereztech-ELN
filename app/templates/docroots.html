Document Roots

Folders the Browse… dialog can open when assigning links

A document root is a folder mounted into the app container (see the volumes: section of docker-compose.yml). Mount your network share or SharePoint sync folder there, then register it here. The optional link prefix rewrites picked paths into the address teammates should open — e.g. container path /shares/rd with prefix https://ereztech.sharepoint.com/sites/RD/Shared%20Documents or \\fileserver\rd.
